Subject: Authorised Representative (Bevollmächtigter) for VerpackG / ElektroG / BattG — quote request (deadline 12 Aug 2026)

Hello,

We are a Japan-based cross-border seller shipping directly to consumers in Germany
via eBay. We need to appoint a German Authorised Representative before the
12 August 2026 deadline, and we would like a quote.

Our current status:
- Packaging (VerpackG): already registered in LUCID.
  Registration number DE1626836528934 (iMak Trading LLC), status "Registered",
  registered 09.04.2026, intra-year volume report submitted.
  No authorised representative appointed yet.
- WEEE (ElektroG) and Batteries (BattG): not yet assessed. Some of our products are
  wristwatches that contain batteries, so we expect at least BattG to apply and we
  would like your assessment on ElektroG.

Our volumes are small: we are a low-volume seller and packaging is limited to
shipping cartons, padded envelopes and protective film for individual parcels.

Could you please provide:
1. Annual fee for acting as our Authorised Representative for **packaging (VerpackG)**.
2. Whether you also act as Authorised Representative for **ElektroG (WEEE)** and
   **BattG (batteries)**, and the fee for each.
3. Whether battery-powered wristwatches fall under ElektroG in your assessment,
   or only under BattG.
4. What documents you need from us, and how long the appointment takes to become
   "Accepted" in the LUCID register — we must be compliant by 12 August 2026.
5. Whether any fees are payable to the dual system separately from your service fee.

We would appreciate a reply this week given the deadline.

Best regards,
Takaaki
iMak Trading LLC

## 送る前に決めること

- **年間予算の上限**。相場が分からないので、3社の見積りを見てから判断する
- WEEE / 電池法まで頼むか、梱包法だけにするか
  → 梱包法だけ通しても**電池側で止められると同じこと**なので、まとめて聞くのが安全

## この後の手順 (LUCID 画面の記載どおり)

1. 代理人と**契約を結ぶ** ("must first enter into an agreement")
2. LUCID の `Appoint an authorised representative` から指名申請
3. 代理人が承諾して `Accepted` になる (ZSVR の公開登記に載る)

※ 'Accepted' の代理人は **1社につき1つだけ**。差し替えるには先に解除が要る。
